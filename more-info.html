We are Servite Mission Sisters, consecrated women who have given our lives entirely to God through the profession of the evangelical counsels of poverty, chastity, and obedience.

As members of a Servite secular institute of consecrated life, we live our vocation within society and among the people, bringing the presence of Christ into the realities of everyday life through prayer, service, professional engagement, and apostolic witness. Rooted in the spirituality of the Servite tradition and inspired by the Blessed Virgin Mary under the title of Our Lady of Sorrows, we seek to accompany humanity with compassion, humility, and faithful service.

Our vocation is marked by a consecrated presence integrated into everyday life, bringing the light of Christ into the situations and relationships that shape society. Servite Mission Sisters engage in diverse apostolates, allowing their service to expand and adapt in response to the needs of the people and the realities in which they serve. Their presence extends to healthcare, education, business, civil service, community engagement, pastoral service, and other areas of mission as needs arise.

Our Mission

Our charism is consecrated presence within society through compassionate witness, accompaniment, and service. Our mission is rooted in our consecration and carried out through apostolic works that respond to the spiritual, social, and human needs of the communities we serve.

Servite Mission Sisters engage in diverse ministries including healthcare, education, business, civil service, and community engagement, allowing their service to adapt according to emerging needs and circumstances.

Healthcare

Serving through healthcare and compassionate care for people in need.

Education

Supporting learning, knowledge, and the development of lives.

Community Life

Walking with communities through faith, support, and service.

Apostolate

Serving through professional work, mission, and dedication.

Through these ministries, we seek to bring the presence of Christ into the realities of life and to serve with faith, integrity, and dedication.

A Call to Give Your Life to God

The Servite Family brings together different vocations, each living the same call to consecrated life in distinct but complementary ways.

Each vocation offers a unique way of giving one’s life to God in service, according to the grace and calling given to each person.

Have you ever felt a deeper desire to belong completely to God? A quiet call to serve Him, not away from the world, but within it?

The Servite Mission Sisters are consecrated women who have responded to this call while living and serving within society.

Prayer for Our Mission

Prayer of Consecration for the African Mission and Beyond

O Lord our God, unless You build this work, we labour in vain. We place before You the establishment, growth, and mission of the Servite Mission Sisters.

We commit this mission and all its work to You. Guide every step, direct every decision, and align everything with Your will and Your time.

Build what should be built. Protect what should be protected. Let this mission grow, serve, and remain for Your glory alone.
