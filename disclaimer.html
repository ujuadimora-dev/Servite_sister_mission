The Servite Mission Sisters provide this website for general information, communication, vocation awareness, pastoral engagement, mission support and public education. While reasonable efforts are made to maintain accurate content, the website should not be treated as a substitute for professional, legal, medical, financial, safeguarding, canonical or pastoral advice.

01 General Information

Information published on this website is intended primarily for general informational and communication purposes.

Website content may include information about:

  • The identity of the Servite Mission Sisters.
  • Spirituality and religious vocation.
  • Discernment and formation.
  • Pastoral and missionary activities.
  • Education and healthcare support.
  • Welfare and community outreach.
  • Donations and public support.
  • Events, announcements and publications.

02 Accuracy of Information

We aim to provide information that is accurate, helpful and current.

However, information may occasionally:

  • Contain errors or omissions.
  • Become outdated.
  • Require correction or clarification.
  • Change without immediate website updates.
  • Differ from local or regional procedures.

Important information should be confirmed directly with an authorised representative of the Servite Mission Sisters.

03 No Guarantee

The website and its content are provided on a general informational basis.

We do not guarantee that:

  • The website will always be available.
  • Every page will be free from errors.
  • All information will remain current.
  • Every external link or embedded service will continue to function.
  • The website will be free from all technical problems or security risks.

04 Religious and Spiritual Content

Prayers, reflections, Scripture quotations, spiritual guidance and vocation materials are provided for religious education and spiritual encouragement.

They do not replace:

  • Personal spiritual direction.
  • Pastoral counselling.
  • Sacramental preparation.
  • Canonical consultation.
  • Formal discernment with authorised vocation personnel.
  • Direct guidance from an appropriate Church authority.

05 Vocation and Formation Information

Information about vocation, discernment, candidacy and formation is provided as a general introduction.

Submission of a vocation enquiry does not guarantee:

  • Acceptance into a formation programme.
  • Admission to the Institute.
  • A formal invitation or appointment.
  • Financial support or sponsorship.
  • Confirmation that a person has a vocation to this particular form of consecrated life.

Every vocation enquiry may be considered individually according to the Institute’s discernment, suitability and formation procedures.

06 Medical and Healthcare Information

Website content relating to health, healthcare, wellbeing, medical outreach or patient support is provided for general information.

It is not intended to:

  • Diagnose a medical condition.
  • Recommend specific treatment.
  • Replace professional medical advice.
  • Replace emergency medical care.
  • Create a patient-and-healthcare-provider relationship.
Anyone experiencing a medical emergency should contact the appropriate emergency service or qualified healthcare professional immediately.

07 Safeguarding Information

General safeguarding information on this website does not replace immediate reporting to the appropriate emergency, child-protection, adult-safeguarding or law-enforcement authority.

Website forms and ordinary email should not be used as the only means of reporting an urgent safeguarding emergency.

Further information is available in our Safeguarding Policy .

08 Financial and Donation Information

Information about donations, payments and mission support is provided for general guidance.

Nothing on the website should be treated as:

  • Financial advice.
  • Tax advice.
  • Investment advice.
  • Accounting advice.
  • A guarantee that a donation is tax-deductible.

Donors should seek appropriate independent advice concerning tax, accounting or legal consequences.

Further information is available in our Donation Policy .
