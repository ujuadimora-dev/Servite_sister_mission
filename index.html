Our Vocation

  • The vocation of a Servite Mission Sister is a call to belong completely to God through a life of consecration, prayer, and apostolic service.
  • Through the profession of the evangelical counsels of poverty, chastity, and obedience, members offer their lives to God in loving service to the Church and the world
  • SMS vocation is nurtured through prayer, formation, community life, and faithful commitment to the mission entrusted to the Institute.
  • If you feel drawn to a deeper relationship with Christ and desire to dedicate your life to God, we invite you to explore SMS vocation through prayer, discernment, and accompaniment.

Discernment Process

The Servite Mission Sisters’ discernment process follows a number of stages designed to help a woman prayerfully and responsibly explore her vocation.

Initial Enquiry

A candidate who feels drawn to the vocation contacts the Servite Mission Sisters and begins a period of informal communication and accompaniment.

Vocation Conversations

Through regular meetings, correspondence, and prayerful reflection, the candidate and the vocation coordinator explore her motivations, life circumstances, spiritual journey, and suitability for the vocation.

Mutual Discernment

Discernment is a shared process. The candidate seeks to understand whether God is calling her to this way of life, while the Institute prayerfully considers whether she possesses the qualities and dispositions necessary for membership. Candidacy

Candidacy

Candidates who demonstrate a genuine interest and suitability may be invited to enter the Candidacy Programme, where they begin a more structured period of discernment and formation.

Admission to Formation

Following a positive period of discernment, suitable candidates may be admitted to the formal stages of formation within the Institute.

Throughout the process, emphasis is placed on prayer, spiritual growth, personal maturity, openness to God’s will, and a deeper understanding of the charism, mission, and way of life of the Servite Mission Sisters.

Learn More About Discernment

Journey of Formation

Formation is a gradual process of spiritual growth, human maturity, and deeper understanding of the vocation and charism of the Institute. It prepares candidates to respond generously to God’s call and to embrace a life of consecration and mission.

Stage One

Candidacy (1–3 Years)

A period of introduction, discernment, and initial formation, during which candidates become familiar with the spirituality, mission, and way of life of the Institute.

Stage Two

Formation and Preparation Period (2–3 Years)

A time of deeper formation, spiritual growth, and integration of the evangelical counsels into daily life, accompanied by increasing participation in the life and mission of the Institute.

Stage Three

Profession

The formal commitment to God through the profession of the evangelical counsels of poverty, chastity, and obedience, lived according to the constitutions and spirituality of the Institute

UNITED IN ONE CHARISM

ServiteFamily

The Servite Family is made up of different vocations, each living the same spiritual heritage and mission in unique but complementary ways.

Servite Friars
Servite Monks
Servite Contemplative Nuns
Servite Religious Sisters
Servite Secular Institute Sisters/ Memebers
Lay Servite Members

✝️ Servite Friars

Religious men who live the Servite vocation through community life, prayer, pastoral ministry, preaching, and service to the Church.

⛪ Servite Monks

We, the Servite Monks, follow Christ in the spirit of Mary, Servant of the Lord. Through prayer, community life and ministry, we strive to make known the love of God, especially to those who suffer.

🙏Servite Contemplative Nuns

We, the Servite Contemplative Nuns, belong to the Institute of the Servants of Mary. We are called to a life of contemplative prayer, silence, and consecration, offering our lives for the Church and the world, especially for those who suffer. In union with Mary, Mother of Sorrows, we stand beneath the Cross with love and hope.

👥 Servite Religious Sisters

We, the Servite Religious Sisters, consecrate our lives to God in the world, following Christ in the spirit of Mary. We commit ourselves to prayer, community and service, bringing God’s love and compassion to all.

👥 Servite Secular Institute Sisters/ Memebers

Consecrated women who live the Servite charism within society and among the people through prayer, consecration, professional engagement, apostolic service, and Christian witness. The Servite Mission Sisters are the African Regional expression of this form of consecrated life.

❤️ Lay Servite Members

Lay Servite Order / Secular Order Lay men and women who live the Servite spirituality according to their state of life while remaining fully engaged in family, professional, and social responsibilities.
